State/UT public-private cesarean delivery gaps

The all_narrow figure presents all eligible births; first_delivery is the subgroup companion. Each is supplied as PNG and vector PDF. Points show private minus public proportions in percentage points, with marginal 95% uncertainty intervals where estimable. Read the legends for sparse samples and withheld intervals. Wave-specific geographies are retained separately; these plots do not establish geographic comparability or test changes between surveys. Estimates are descriptive and unadjusted.

Figures were drawn from the saved aggregate CSV; analytical models were not rerun.

To reproduce with pandas and matplotlib installed:
python plot_statewise.py --input statewise_primary_results.csv --output .
