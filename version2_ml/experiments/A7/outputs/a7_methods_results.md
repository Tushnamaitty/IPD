## Deviations and caveats (post-run documentation review)

This section was added after the run; rerunning the generator would overwrite
it with the original template.

- **Threshold.** Thresholded metrics (precision, recall, F1) use the fixed
  prespecified 0.50 threshold, not a validation-selected threshold. This is a
  deviation from handoff sections 4 and 7. They are not comparable across
  calibration states; ROC-AUC, AP and Brier are the appropriate comparisons.
- **Calibration encoding.** `aggregate_model_comparison.csv` has no
  `calibration_method` column. For each of the six model/tier candidates, the
  three validation rows are in fixed order: uncalibrated, Platt, isotonic.
  This was verified against `calibration_summary.csv` by matching Brier scores
  for all six candidates.
- **Facility SHAP.** `F1_detail_history` contains both `facility_type` and
  `facility_detail`; combined facility share is 0.4148 overall (41.5%; fold
  range 0.4114-0.4188; residence subgroups 0.4011 urban and 0.4177 rural) for
  XGBoost. The tier's ROC-AUC/AP uplift bundles facility detail with observed
  history; A7 does not decompose it. A4's matched ablations provide that
  decomposition.
- A1 state features are not in any A7 tier, so A7 cannot speak to location
  context.
- **F0.** The F0 row is uncalibrated, uses raw probabilities at 0.50, is not
  comparable to calibrated rows, and is a reconciled 36-feature reference
  fitted with A7 parameters, not a reproduction of the V1 score.
- **Test scoring.** The recording run scored the test partition once; a
  sandbox verification run scored it a second time on 2026-10-19. Values were
  identical, no decision depended on the second scoring, and the committed
  snapshot is the original recording.
- **Model comparisons.** Between-model differences are smaller than
  split-to-split variation for ROC-AUC in both tiers (0.0033-0.0056 versus
  0.0075-0.0081) and for AP in `F1_detail_history` (0.0066 versus 0.0128).
  The `F1` AP gap for logistic regression (~0.026) is larger than the
  repeated-split AP range (~0.009). No model is declared superior.
- **History quality.** `history_quality_flag` is constant in validation: all
  validation rows are `ok`, and the 14 ambiguous cohort rows fall outside
  validation. Its SHAP share is 0.0000, so it contributes no validation split
  signal.
- **Robustness rows.** The table below reports the tier and calibration state
  used by each `a7_robustness.csv` line. All prediction lines were evaluated at
  threshold 0.50, although the reported ROC-AUC, AP and Brier do not depend on
  it. Because calibration state differs, Brier scores are not directly
  comparable between the uncalibrated repeated-split/sensitivity lines and the
  calibrated subgroup lines; ROC-AUC and AP are rank-based.
